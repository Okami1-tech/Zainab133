You are not a distant dream to me.

You are the person I want to make these intentions real with. I miss you, I long to be beside you, and I want the ordinary parts of life with you just as much as the special ones.

"I need you as much as I need oxygen to breathe. I don't say that to put a burden on you; I say it because that's how deeply I long for a life with you."

And I want that love to make me more responsible, more patient, more hardworking and closer to Allah.

05 / MY DUA

Ya Allah, make it good for us.

"If this marriage is good for our deen, our lives and our akhirah, make its path easy, bless our efforts, provide for us from where we do not expect, and unite us in a marriage filled with sakinah, mercy and barakah."
